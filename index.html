OpenX Studio — ASAM OpenSCENARIO & OpenDRIVE Simulator

In-browser scenario player, visual editor, and 3D simulator for autonomous driving scenarios.

Features & Capabilities

  • Zero Install: Full esmini C++ simulation engine running in WebAssembly (WASM).
  • Standards Compliance: Supports ASAM OpenSCENARIO (.xosc) and OpenDRIVE (.xodr).
  • High-Performance 3D: Three.js viewport with Orbit, Follow Ego, and Top-Down camera perspectives.
  • Web Worker Concurrency: Off-main-thread stepping for smooth 60+ FPS UI responsiveness.
  • Inspector & Telemetry: Real-time speed (km/h, m/s), coordinates, vehicle parameters, and storyboards.
